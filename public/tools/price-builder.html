What each entry means

Variable cost per unit

Costs that occur every time you make one more sale — raw materials, ingredients, direct labour on that unit, packaging and payment-processing fees.

ExampleOne photo-studio booking costs 50 MDL in cleaning, electricity and wear.

Monthly fixed costs & units

Fixed costs stay the same whether you sell 1 or 1,000 — rent, base salaries, software, insurance. Divided by your expected monthly units, they give the fixed cost carried by each unit.

Example10,000 MDL studio rent ÷ 40 bookings = 250 MDL of fixed cost per booking.

Customer acquisition cost (CAC)

The real cost of getting one paying customer through advertising. Either enter it directly (total ad spend ÷ customers won) or build it from your ad funnel below.

ExampleA consulting client costs €60 to acquire via ads — that €60 must be built into the retainer.

The ad funnel (just count it)

No percentages to work out — just read the numbers off your ad dashboard: how many clicks, how many leads (inquiries), and how many paying customers.

Ad spend = CPC × clicks  ·  CPL = spend ÷ leads  ·  CAC = spend ÷ customers

ExampleCPC 5 MDL × 400 clicks = 2,000 MDL; 20 booked → CAC 100 MDL.

Profit: margin vs. markup

Margin is profit as a share of the price; markup is profit as a share of the cost. A 50% markup is only a 33% margin — the tool shows both so they're never confused.

ExampleCost 70, price 100 → 30% margin = 42.9% markup.

Break-even price & units

The break-even price is fulfillment + CAC — the floor below which you lose money on every sale. Break-even units is how many you must sell monthly to cover fixed costs at your price.

ExampleAt 667 MDL a booking, a studio needs ~22 bookings a month to cover its fixed + ad costs.

Total revenue & profit / month

The per-unit price × your expected units per month gives the money actually flowing through the business: revenue (everything you take in) and profit (what's left after all costs and ads).

Example667 MDL × 40 bookings = 26,667 MDL revenue, ~10,667 MDL profit a month.
Worked example

Load “Photography rental + ads” above: 50 variable + 250 fixed = 300 MDL fulfillment. The funnel — 5 MDL × 400 clicks, 20 bookings — gives a 100 MDL CAC, so the cost floor is 400 MDL. A 40% margin sets the price near 667 MDL a session. Across 40 bookings that's ~26,667 MDL revenue and ~10,667 MDL profit a month. Drop the CAC and watch the price fall — that gap is exactly what new businesses forget to charge for.
